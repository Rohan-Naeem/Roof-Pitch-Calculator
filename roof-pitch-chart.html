3/12 roof pitch

A 3/12 roof pitch rises 3 inches per 12 inches of run. Its angle is 14.0 degrees, its slope is 25 per cent, and its multiplier is 1.031. It is a low pitch that can be shingled with the right underlayment, and it is a common pitch for single-slope and low-slope sections.

4/12 roof pitch

A 4/12 roof pitch rises 4 inches per 12 inches of run. Its angle is 18.4 degrees, its slope is 33.3 per cent, and its multiplier is 1.054. It is one of the most common pitches on United States homes, and it is the lower end of the usual standard range of 4/12 to 9/12.

6/12 roof slope

A 6/12 roof slope, also written as a 6/12 roof pitch, rises 6 inches per 12 inches of run. Its angle is 26.6 degrees, its slope is 50 per cent, and its multiplier is 1.118. It sheds rain and snow well, it is still walkable in dry conditions for an experienced person, and it is a good middle ground between a low roof and a steep roof.
