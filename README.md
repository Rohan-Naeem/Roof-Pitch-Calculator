# Roof Pitch Calculator

Free roof pitch calculator — find pitch, angle, slope, rafter length and roof pitch multiplier from rise over run.

**Live site: https://roofpitchcalculators.vercel.app/**

## What it does

Enter the rise and run of your roof, or a pitch or angle you already know, and the calculator works out:

- Roof pitch in x:12 form (for example 6/12)
- Angle in degrees
- Slope as a percentage
- Rafter length
- Roof pitch multiplier and roof area

It is a planning tool for homeowners, builders and roofers in the United States. Planning estimates only — it does not replace a structural check, a building-code check or a professional roof inspection.
