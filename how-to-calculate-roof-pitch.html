Step by step: how to figure out roof pitch

Step 1 — Measure from inside the attic. This is the safest way. Find a rafter you can reach, hold a spirit level horizontally against its underside, and mark a point 12 inches along the level from the rafter.

Step 2 — Measure the rise. From that mark, measure straight down to the top of the rafter. That vertical distance, in inches, is the first number of your pitch. If it is 6 inches, the pitch is 6/12.

Step 3 — Work out the angle. Divide the rise by the run (6 divided by 12 is 0.5), then take the inverse tangent. The angle for 6/12 is about 26.6 degrees. Use the calculator above, or the roof pitch chart, for the exact figure.

Step 4 — Work out the rafter length. Square the rise, square the run, add them, and take the square root. For rise 6 and run 12, that is the square root of 36 + 144, which is 13.42 inches for a 12-inch run, or 13.42 feet for a 12-foot run.

Measuring from the roof edge

If you cannot get into the attic, you can measure at the eaves, where the roof meets the wall. Use the same 12-inch run and measure the rise over it. Only do this from a ladder you are confident on, and never walk on a roof you are unsure about. If the roof is steep, wet, or damaged, hire a professional to measure it.

Safety: do not walk on a steep roof, a wet roof, or a roof with loose or damaged material. If you are unsure, measure from inside the attic or from the ground, or hire a professional.

Formulas

Pitch = (rise ÷ run) × 12. Angle = arctan(rise ÷ run). Slope % = (rise ÷ run) × 100. Rafter length = √(rise² + run²). Multiplier = rafter length ÷ run.

FAQs

How to calculate roof pitch in degrees?

Measure the rise and the run in the same units, divide the rise by the run, then take the inverse tangent of that figure. In symbols, angle = arctan(rise divided by run). For example, rise 6 and run 12 gives arctan(0.5), which is about 26.6 degrees.

How do you determine the pitch of a roof?

You determine the pitch of a roof by measuring its rise over a 12-inch run. Hold a level horizontally from a rafter, mark 12 inches along it, and measure down to the rafter. That distance in inches is the first number of the pitch.

What is a rafter pitch calculator?

A rafter pitch calculator works out the pitch, angle and rafter length from the rise and run of a roof. The rafter length is the sloping length of the rafter, found with Pythagoras: rafter squared equals rise squared plus run squared.
