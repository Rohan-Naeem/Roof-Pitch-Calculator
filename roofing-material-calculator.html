Roofing Material Calculator

Assumptions stated plainly: 1 roofing square = 100 sq ft; 3 bundles of standard 3-tab shingles per square; one roll of underlayment covers about 400 sq ft. Check the packaging for the exact products you buy, because coverage varies by brand.

Worked example: 40 ft by 30 ft at 6/12 pitch, 10% waste — roof area about 1,476 sq ft, 14.76 squares, 45 bundles, 4 rolls of underlayment.

How to calculate roof area

To calculate roof area, first work out the flat footprint area by multiplying the length of the building by its width. Then multiply that figure by the roof pitch multiplier for your pitch. For a 6/12 pitch the multiplier is 1.118, so a 1,200 square foot footprint becomes about 1,342 square feet of roof. Add a waste allowance — 10 per cent for a simple roof is common — and divide by 100 to get roofing squares. That is the figure suppliers use when they quote materials.

What materials does the estimate cover?

The estimate covers shingle bundles and underlayment rolls, which are the two items that scale directly with roof area. It does not cover ridge caps, flashing, nails, vents or drip edge, because those depend on the shape of the roof, not just its area. Get a full list from your supplier or roofer before you order.
